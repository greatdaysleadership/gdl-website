A Change-Management Engagement

Navigate

For organizations moving through change, with care for the people who will live it out.

Our Point of View

Transitions are systems, not events.

The announcement, the launch day, the org chart that goes out: that is what everyone sees, and it is 5% of the change. The other 95% is the structured work that surrounds it. Most organizations underinvest in that work, then wonder why the launch went sideways.

PEOPLE

Who lives the change: successors, teams, and the middle layer.

PROCESS

How the change moves through the organization, step by step.

PACE

The tempo the organization can actually absorb.

Sound Familiar?

Six things we hear when change is already moving.

Navigate is built for organizations moving through significant change: a succession, a merger, a restructuring, an AI shift, an expansion, or a growth chapter that demands a new scale.

Our founder leaves in six weeks. The succession conversation started last month.

Thirty years of context about to walk out; a successor named but not prepared.

We announced the merger by email. People complied. Then the resumes started moving.

A workforce that is compliant, exhausted, and quietly looking.

Nobody mapped this culture for me. I find the landmines by stepping on them.

A new CEO spending the first 100 days burning political capital on avoidable mistakes.

We doubled in size. Our managers are still running the old company.

A growth chapter that demands a new scale, and the middle of the org chart was never prepared for it.

We announced our AI strategy before we asked what the workforce could absorb.

No honest read on what work changes or what new skills the leaders will need to build.

The deal closed months ago. The integration plan is still at 50,000 feet.

The deal team did its job; the people work was never mapped.

What Changes

What is different at close.

Every bearing moves the organization from bracing for change to actually being ready for it.

The Book Behind the Firm

The principles behind every engagement.

Written by Dr. Matt Paden with Dr. L. Ken Jones, The Core cuts through the noise of quick-fix leadership strategies to reveal what actually sustains effective leadership. Those eight principles anchor every engagement we run.

Members of the firm have spent decades working with senior leaders in healthcare, faith-led nonprofits, higher education, and founder-led companies, the institutions where the cost of fragmented leadership development is most visible and most personal.

The Method

One system. One method. Three levers.

Every engagement runs on the same methodology. What changes is the part of the leadership system it strengthens.

1

Anchor

Clarity

Diagnose honestly. Assessment and interviews that name what is actually happening before anything is prescribed.

2

Architect

Competency

Design deliberately. A plan built for your context, your cohort, and your calendar, not a program off the shelf.

3

Activate

Chemistry

Build the rhythm. Working sessions, coaching, and cadence that embed the change instead of announcing it.

How We Work

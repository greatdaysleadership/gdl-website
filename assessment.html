The statements

Rate each statement 1 to 5. Each one describes what a 1, 3, and 5 look like, so you are matching a picture, not guessing a number.

How the scale works

Every statement shows what a 1, a 3, and a 5 look like for that specific question. A 2 means between the 1 and the 3; a 4 means between the 3 and the 5. For the exact meaning of any number, hover over it. On a phone, tap a number and its meaning appears under the statement so you can adjust if it does not match.

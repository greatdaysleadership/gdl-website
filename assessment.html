The statements

Rate each statement 1 to 5. The scale rewards evidence: a 5 means you could point to examples from the past month.

The scale
  • 1 Not true of us. I can't recall this happening.
  • 2 Rarely true. Happens occasionally, not by design.
  • 3 Sometimes true. Depends on the person or the week.
  • 4 Usually true, with real exceptions.
  • 5 Consistently true. I could point to examples from the past month.

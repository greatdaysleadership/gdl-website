About the Book

It begins at the core.

In a world overflowing with leadership seminars, self-help guides, and quick-fix strategies, The Core cuts through the noise to reveal what truly sustains effective leadership. Every leader reaches a moment when skill isn't enough, when the challenge cuts deeper and tests conviction, humility, and heart.

Through the journey of a young man whose plans are upended by tragedy, The Core blends a compelling story of mentorship with timeless principles. Under the guidance of Dr. Bill Jackson, a hospital CEO who leads with quiet strength, Clint Smith discovers that great leadership grows from the inside out.

Part fable, part field guide, accessible and deeply practical: leadership isn't for the few. It's for all of us, and it begins at the core.

About the Authors

Meet the Authors

Dr. Matt Paden

Co-Author

Dr. Matt Paden is the President and Managing Partner of Great Days Leadership, where he helps leaders and organizations grow with purpose, integrity, and impact. With more than two decades of experience as an executive, consultant, speaker, and coach, Matt specializes in aligning teams around mission, building healthy cultures, and developing clear, values-driven leadership. He holds a doctorate in organizational leadership from Pepperdine University and lives in Franklin, Tennessee, with his wife and their two children.

KJ

Dr. L. Ken Jones

Co-Author

Dr. L. Ken Jones is President of Oklahoma Christian University. His career spans engineering (Ph.D., Oklahoma State University), pulpit preaching, leadership training, and more than four decades in Christian higher education, including nineteen years as President of Lubbock Christian University. He and his wife Suzie have two grown children and four grandchildren.

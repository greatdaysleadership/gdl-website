Our Point of View

Alignment is three things moving together.

Not a poster, not an offsite. Each lever can look healthy on its own; misalignment lives in the gaps between them. Most firms diagnose them separately. We treat them as one system, because that is how your organization experiences them.

Align is built for executive teams in organizations of 25 to 500 people, where the pieces are all present and the direction is not.

Everyone signed off on the strategy. Nobody is operating against it.

Leadership teams that agree in the room and diverge in practice.

The values on the wall are not the values in the hallway.
